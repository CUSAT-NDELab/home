Research

From the right sensor to the right alarm at the right time — our research covers the full diagnostics pipeline.

Focus areas

Six areas where we push the boundary

Each area combines experimental work on our test rigs with modelling and machine learning.

Structural Health Monitoring

Vestibulum ante ipsum primis in faucibus. Sensor networks and modal analysis for civil and mechanical structures.

Machinery Fault Diagnosis

Detecting and localising bearing, gear and rotor faults from vibration and acoustic signatures.

Prognostics & RUL

Data-driven and physics-informed models to estimate remaining useful life of critical components.

Smart Sensing Systems

Low-cost, edge-capable sensor nodes and data-acquisition for field deployment.

Signal Processing

Time-frequency, wavelet and empirical decomposition methods for non-stationary signals.

Reliability & Safety

Reliability modelling and risk assessment for engineered assets and infrastructure.
